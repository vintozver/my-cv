# Personal statement

I want to keep growing—and help create the conditions in which others can grow, too.

- **Keep learning.** A growth mindset means staying curious, learning from experience,
  and remaining open to new perspectives.
- **Put life before price.** Every life is precious and has no currency value. Human
  dignity and wellbeing should never be reduced to a measure of profit or success.
- **Make room for everyone.** Everyone deserves a fair opportunity to learn, contribute,
  and grow—in a safe environment protected from harm and discrimination.
- **Choose cooperation.** Competition is part of the world we live in, but collaboration
  and shared effort can take us further than separation and segregation.
- **Care for our shared home.** We should value the natural environment, its resources,
  and the people with whom we share it.
- **Protect what sustains life.** Nature and its resources are precious. If we cannot
  breathe clean air or eat, we cannot live; caring for our environment is essential.
