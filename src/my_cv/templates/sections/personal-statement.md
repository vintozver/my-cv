# Personal statement

I believe in a growth mindset and in the preciousness of every life: life has no
currency value. Everyone deserves a fair opportunity to grow and a safe environment,
free from harm and discrimination.

We live in a competitive world, but cooperation and collaboration are stronger than
separation and segregation. We should value our environment, our home, and one another.

Our natural environment and resources are precious and must be cared for. Without clean
air to breathe or food to eat, we cannot live.
